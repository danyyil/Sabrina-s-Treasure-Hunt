The Final Revelation
The road behind you now is done,
The final treasure has begun.
One last trail lies swift and straight,
And somewhere there the answers wait.

Do not seek a sign or stone—
The final clue will be your own.

Race the one who sent you here,
And at the finish, look quite near.

Upon his bib, the final thread
Awaits beneath the words you've read.
